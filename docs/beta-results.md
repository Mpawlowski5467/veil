## Recording a result

Record an anonymous report identifier or a public issue link, Veil/client/OS
versions, authentication route, completed exercises, time to first verification,
maintainer assistance, and blockers. Mark untried steps explicitly. Obtain the
participant's permission before quoting them; do not copy personal logs or data.

Group findings by install, routing/verification, detection, review burden,
editing/resume/concurrency, recovery/compaction, upgrades, and removal. Preserve
failed journeys in totals. A successful automated local check and an echoed
email alone are not verified client journeys.
